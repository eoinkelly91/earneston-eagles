Club Officers

Chairman

Robert Mitchell

Oversees the club's vision, steering the Eagles toward a successful, community-focused future.

"Our club is built on passion and community. My focus is on ensuring we continue to provide a great, inclusive space for everyone to thrive."

Secretary

Clare Davis

The administrative heart of the club, managing registrations, matchday logistics and league communications.

"Keeping the Eagles running smoothly behind the scenes is busy, but seeing our teams walk out on matchday makes every bit of paperwork worth it."

Treasurer

David Walsh

Ensures every penny is carefully budgeted and reinvested into our teams and facilities.

"My priority is keeping us financially sustainable so we can continue to invest in our players, volunteers, and the ground we call home."
