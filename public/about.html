We Are The Eagles

The Earneston Eagles are more than just a sports club—we are a tight-knit community united by passion, dedication, and sportsmanship. From grassroots youth development to our competitive senior teams, our mission is to provide an inclusive, positive environment where athletes of all abilities can develop their skills, build lasting friendships, and wear the purple and gold with pride. Rooted in local support and driven by our dedicated volunteers, the Eagles are committed to fostering athletic excellence both on and off the field.

Our History

Founded by local sports enthusiasts who wanted to bring competitive team sport to the local community, the Earneston Eagles began as a modest squad playing on shared community pitches. Over the decades, through tireless community fundraising and the passion of generations of players, coaches, and supporters, the club steadily expanded—eventually establishing our own home ground with a seated stand and floodlit facilities. Today, the Eagles proudly honor that founding spirit of grit and teamwork as we continue to build a legacy for future generations.

Join Us

Whether you're looking to take the pitch as a player, share your knowledge as a coach, lend a hand as a volunteer, or simply cheer on the team under the Friday night lights, there is always a place for you with the Eagles. We welcome new members across all age grades and skill levels, as well as local partners looking to support community sports. Get in touch with our committee today or visit our clubhouse to become part of the Earneston Eagles family!
